About RFI

Your Gateway to Quality
Indian Manufacturing

At Resources From India, we believe international trade should be transparent, reliable, and mutually beneficial. Our mission is to simplify global sourcing by connecting international buyers with trusted Indian manufacturers.

Who We Are

RFI is a premier B2B sourcing platform that connects international buyers with verified Indian manufacturers and suppliers. We bridge the cultural and logistical gaps to create seamless trading experiences.

Our Mission

To simplify global sourcing by building trusted relationships between international buyers and Indian suppliers, enabling businesses worldwide to access quality products at competitive prices.

Our Values

Trust, transparency, and long-term partnerships form the foundation of everything we do. We believe successful business relationships are built on integrity, communication, and mutual growth.
