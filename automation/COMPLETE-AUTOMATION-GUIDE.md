## 📱 STEP 1: LINKEDIN AUTOMATION (EASY SETUP)

**Setup:**
1. Go to https://buffer.com
2. Sign up FREE
3. Connect LinkedIn Company Page
4. Start adding posts from `content/content-calendar.md`

**How to Add Posts:**
```
1. Open Buffer Dashboard
2. Click "Create Post"
3. Paste content from calendar
4. Add images (use Canva for graphics)
5. Schedule for optimal time
6. Repeat daily!
```

## 🔍 STEP 2: SEO & GOOGLE SETUP

### Google Analytics (Track Your Visitors)

1. Go to: https://analytics.google.com
2. Click "Start for free"
3. Create account with your email
4. Setup property: "RFI Website"
5. Get your Tracking ID (looks like: G-XXXXXXXXXX)
6. Replace `YOUR_GA_ID` in index.html

### Google Search Console (Get Indexed)

1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add your website
3. Verify ownership (HTML tag method)
4. Submit sitemap: `https://resourcesfromindia.github.io/ri-global/sitemap.xml`
